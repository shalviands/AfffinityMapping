# INCUBX Affinity Tool — User Guide

Welcome to the **INCUBX Affinity Mapping Tool**! 

Affinity Mapping is a qualitative research technique used by startup founders to take chaotic, messy user interviews and systematically structure them into clear, actionable themes. Our tool automates the tedious transcription and clustering phases, allowing you to focus purely on analysis and execution.

---

## 🗺️ How to Use the Platform

### Step 1: Start a New Session
When conducting a stakeholder interview, begin by clicking **Start Session**. 
You'll map out your specific "Research Question" (e.g. *Why are early adopters churning after 3 days?*). This forces the AI engine to specifically look for insights that answer your core business problem!

### Step 2: Upload Your Research
Once set up, you have three ways to import data:
1. **Live Record:** Open a direct microphone channel. Bhashini AI will process mixed-lingual regional Indian dialects natively into English.
2. **File Upload:** Drag your .mp3 or .m4a interview files directly onto the drop-zone.
3. **Text Paste:** If you already have Zoom logs, simply paste the transcript formatting it as `Speaker 1:` and `Speaker 2:`.

### Step 3: Card Review 
Rather than forcing you to read massive paragraphs, our AI engine digests the transcript and extracts **"Core Insights"** into bite-sized qualitative cards! 
- *Too long?* Click the **Scissors** icon to split a card into two distinct thoughts.
- *Irrelevant?* Click the **Trash** icon to discard it.
- Once you've groomed the list, hit **Confirm & Start Clustering**.

### Step 4: The Affinity Board (The Magic)
This is where analysis happens!
- **Dragging:** Click and drag cards to group them into themed columns (Clusters).
- **Sub-Grouping:** Sub-divide large columns to find micro-patterns.
- **Naming Constraints:** Once an organic theme forms, use the Dropdown Menu `(...)` on the cluster to dynamically rename it! 
- **AI Synthesis:** Pop open the **AI Synthesis Bar** on any cluster to read an AI-generated paragraph explaining exactly why this cluster is important to your startup's sector.
- **Guided Sidebar:** Feeling lost? Toggle "Guided Mode" at the top so the system acts as a virtual mentor, analyzing your board state and telling you what to do next.

### Step 5: Prioritization & Export
Don't solve every problem; solve the critical ones!
Click the **Priority Matrix** button. The engine procedurally plots all your clusters on a visual graph analyzing **Card Frequency vs. Negative Sentiments**. Focus entirely on the "Act First" red quadrant!

Once satisfied, click **Export** to generate instant presentation-ready summaries (PDF / CSV / Lean Canvas Nodes) straight to your desktop.

---

## 💡 Pro Tips
- Notice a yellow **"Conflict"** badge on a column? That means the AI noticed that your users completely disagree over that specific topic. Click the badge to deploy a resolution panel that lets you mark "Primary signals" to avoid muddying your data.
- If you accidentally delete a critical card out of a stack, quickly look to the bottom of the screen! An **Undo Bar** tracks every action for 5 seconds offering a quick reversal.
